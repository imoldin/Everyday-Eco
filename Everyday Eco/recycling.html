Waste & Recycling

Waste is not just rubbish. It is materials, energy and effort that we can often use again. Learn the 3 Rules and how to cut waste all week long.

The 3 Rules

Follow them in order: reduce first, recycle last.

1. Reduce

Use less in the first place. The best waste is the waste that never exists.

2. Reuse

Give items another life before throwing them away: jars, bags, clothes and boxes.

3. Recycle

When an item cannot be reduced or reused, recycle it correctly so the material can be recycled and used again.

Everyday waste habits

Avoid single-use products

Swap disposable items for lasting ones.

  • Use a reusable cup, cutlery and bag.
  • Choose refillable products.

Reuse old items

Many things can be used again.

  • Turn jars into storage.
  • Pass on clothes and furniture.

Sort recyclables correctly

Rules differ by area, so check your local council guidance.

  • Rinse containers and keep them clean.
  • Keep non-recyclables out of the recycling bin.

Repair, don`t replace

A repair keeps materials in use for longer.

  • Sew a button or patch a tear.
  • Look for a local repair shop.

Compost food scraps

Suitable scraps like vegetable peelings can become compost for plants.

  • Check what your compost bin or local service accepts.

Reduce waste through the week

One small focus for each day.

Monday

Plan your week`s meals and write a shopping list.

Tuesday

Take a reusable cup or bottle wherever you go.

Wednesday

Check the bin: what could be reused or recycled?

Thursday

Try a repair instead of buying new.

Friday

Say no to one piece of unneeded packaging.

Saturday

Sort your recycling and compost food scraps.

Sunday

Use up leftovers and review your week.
